Lassi

Home

Description

Lassi is a refreshing drink made throughout the Indian Subcontinent

Ingredients

  • Yoghurt
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Spices, Other Condiments (optional)

Steps

  1. Pour yoghurt and water in the blender
  2. Add sugar and salt to taste
  3. Add condiments if needed to taste
  4. Turn on blender
  5. Drink and enjoy
