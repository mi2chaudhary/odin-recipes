Description

Rooh Afza is a deep red syrup often drunk in summers after mixing it in water. It does not require much work to make, and is very tasty.

Ingredients

  • Water
  • Rooh Afza Syrup

Steps

  1. Add Rooh Afza Syrup
  2. Stir well
  3. Drink and enjoy
