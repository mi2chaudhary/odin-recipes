Description

Lemonade is a cooling drink made primarily from lemons that is popular across the world in multiple variations.

Ingredients

  • Lemons
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Water

Steps

  1. Squeeze your lemons to get lemon juice
  2. Add water
  3. Add sugar and salt to taste
  4. Mix well
  5. Drink and enjoy
